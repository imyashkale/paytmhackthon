AI TEAMMATES - TOP 5 BUSINESS USE CASES
=======================================

AI Teammates gives AI agents permission to take real actions in your CRM,
billing, ticketing, calendar, knowledge base and messaging tools, within
set limits.


1. INSTANT REFUNDS AND PAYMENT DISPUTES
---------------------------------------
Problem:        Customers wait days for a refund or a failed-transaction
                reversal. Support agents spend most of their time checking
                orders against policy and doing the same steps again and again.
What changes:   The AI checks the order, checks the refund policy, issues the
                refund, updates the ticket and tells the customer, all in
                minutes. Refunds above a set amount (Rs 5,000 by default)
                still go to a human for approval.
Business value: Faster refunds mean fewer complaints, fewer chargebacks and
                less customer churn. It also means fewer support staff are
                needed for routine cases.


2. FOLLOWING UP EVERY SALES LEAD RIGHT AWAY
-------------------------------------------
Problem:        Leads (for example, merchants signing up for payment devices,
                payment gateway or loans) go cold because no one follows up
                for hours or days.
What changes:   When a lead arrives, the AI looks it up in the CRM, scores it,
                books a meeting with a sales rep and sends a follow-up,
                without anyone stepping in.
Business value: More leads become customers, sales reps spend their time only
                on good leads, and no lead is missed.


3. HANDLING ROUTINE SUPPORT QUESTIONS AUTOMATICALLY
---------------------------------------------------
Problem:        Most tickets are repeat questions: "Where's my money?",
                "Why was I charged?", "How do I raise my limit?"
What changes:   The AI reads the policy and pricing documents, answers the
                customer, resolves the ticket, and sends only the unusual
                cases to a person.
Business value: Cheaper support per ticket, round-the-clock coverage without
                adding staff, and the same answer every time.


4. KEEPING CUSTOMERS THROUGH BILLING GOODWILL
---------------------------------------------
Problem:        Customers who were overcharged or hit by a failed transaction
                leave quietly. Giving them a goodwill credit takes several
                layers of manual approval.
What changes:   The AI spots the billing problem, applies a credit within
                approved limits, and confirms by SMS, email or Slack.
Business value: You fix the problem before the customer leaves, protect
                revenue, and spend less on refunds in bulk because each credit
                is targeted.


5. SAFE AUTOMATION WITH CLEAR OVERSIGHT
---------------------------------------
Problem:        Leadership wants AI to save money but won't let it touch money
                or customer data without controls.
What changes:   Every action is logged, risky actions need human approval, and
                access is controlled. A status dashboard shows the health of
                every connected system.
Business value: Risk and compliance teams can approve AI use more easily
                (important for a regulated payments company), and it's clear
                who did what. The same setup can then be extended to other
                teams like collections, KYC and merchant onboarding without
                starting over.


ONE-LINE PITCH
--------------
AI Teammates turn hours of manual sales and support work into minutes of
automated, rule-bound action, with humans approving only the high-stakes cases.
